Get your Team plan link

You type your email, you get a link, you click it. The link switches on the Team plan in the browser — and it is the same link you pass on to the rest of the team.
There is no sign-up, no password and no card. The email does one job: prove the mailbox is yours, and give us somewhere to send the renewal. It is the closest thing to a login this product will ever have, and it happens outside the tool — inside it nothing changed: no video, audio or document leaves your computer.

If your company already subscribed, the link arrives with the plan you bought and with the client name. If not, it arrives as a 14-day trial — once per email.

What a block does, and what it does not. It stops the next issuance — the key already in that person’s browser keeps working until it expires. There is no instant cut, and there will not be: for that, the tool would have to ask a server for permission to work, and it would then stop working on a client machine with the network locked down, which is exactly where it needs to work. The term above is your real control: at 7 days, a cut bites within a week; at 90, it can take three months.

How it works underneath

  1. You ask. Type your email here. Nothing is decided at this moment: a message goes out to your mailbox, that is all.
  2. You click what arrived. That is what proves the address is yours — and it is the only moment anything resembling a session exists.
  3. You come back with the link ready. The key is signed on the server, with your email inside it, and handed over already wrapped in a Walkstamp address.
  4. From then on, no network at all. The key is checked inside the browser, against a public key that sits in the tool's own HTML. It works on a plane, on the client machine with the internet locked down, and on the computer IT will not let talk to our hosting.

Why the validity is short

Each key issued lasts 90 days (14 on the trial), and the next one arrives by email before the previous expires. This is not bureaucracy: it is what makes a leaked link die on its own. Since no server checks anything while you work, there is no revoke button — the deadline is the revocation. It is a trade we chose on purpose: we would rather have a leak that expires than a paid product that stops working when the internet drops.

What is kept

Your email, the plan it is entitled to, and the date of the last issue. That is all, and only so we can renew and notice odd use. The key itself is not stored — it is signed and handed over. Nothing you record, transcribe or generate passes through here: see the security page and the privacy policy.
