This Annex supplements the {{marca}} Terms of Use and describes, for the organisation that uses the tool (the “Customer”), how personal data is processed in that use. It is offered by {{empresa}}, company register (CNPJ) {{cnpj}} (the “Provider”). {{marca}} is free of charge and has no account.

The point that defines this Annex. The Provider does not process personal data on behalf of the Customer. {{marca}} runs entirely in the user's browser: video, audio, screen images, transcripts and generated documents are never transmitted to the Provider. There is no processing server, no account and no Customer database. Vendor-review questions about retention, encryption or destruction of that content do not apply, because it never reaches the Provider — and the Customer can verify this itself with the browser's network inspector open on the Network tab.

1. Roles

  1. The Customer is the controller of what it records and processes with the tool: it decides what is recorded, for what purpose and on what legal basis, and is responsible for informing the people who appear in the recordings.
  2. The Provider does not receive, access or store that content, and therefore does not act as a processor of it (LGPD art. 5, VII). It provides the software, which the Customer runs in its own browser.
  3. The Provider acts as controller, in its own name, only of the data described in its Privacy Policy — the technical access data the site's hosting receives, the aggregate visit count and the e-mails it receives. That data is outside the scope of this Annex.
  4. No Customer content is used to train artificial intelligence models, the Provider's own or third parties': it never reaches the Provider.

2. Where processing happens

  1. Content of the recordings — screens, audio, transcripts, annotations and generated documents: processed in the user's browser, on the Customer's device. It does not leave it, except through the optional features described in section 3, triggered by the user.
  2. Settings kept in the browser — preferences, templates, client brand, the “Issued by” field and, if the user ticks the option, the vocabulary list: kept in the browser's local storage, under the Customer's control, and erased when the site data is cleared in the browser.
  3. Copy of a recording in progress — the captured screens are mirrored to the browser's private file storage (Origin Private File System), so that a crash does not lose the work. The copy is deleted when a document is generated, after seven days or when the user discards it.

4. Customer responsibilities

  1. To have a legal basis for the recordings and to inform the people recorded, as applicable law requires.
  2. Not to capture unnecessary personal data, and to use the tool's redaction and cropping features before sharing a document.
  3. To look after the devices and browsers on which the tool is used: what it keeps stays in that browser's storage until the site data is cleared.
  4. To decide which optional features — Google Drive, Google Docs, sharing — are allowed in its environment.

5. Technical measures

  1. Minimisation by architecture: processing is local, in the browser. The strongest security measure in this Annex is the absence of the data on the Provider's side.
  2. No application server, account or Customer database: the site and the tool are served as pages by the hosting.
  3. In transit: the site, the tool and the downloads of libraries and models use an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  4. Local storage under the Customer's control: nothing the tool keeps in the browser is sent to the Provider; the copy of a recording in progress is deleted under the conditions of section 2.
  5. Offline version: the single file the Customer downloads contains none of the external addresses in section 3, and can be kept, scanned and versioned by the Customer.
  6. Google: drive.file scope, limited to the files chosen by the user and those created by the tool, and explicit confirmation before any sending.

The Provider does not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification and does not claim to.

6. Security incidents

As the Provider keeps no Customer content, an incident on its side cannot expose that content. If the Provider becomes aware of an incident affecting the site or the tool that may cause relevant risk or harm to the Customer — for example, an unauthorised change to the code served —, it will publish a notice on the site and make the notifications required by LGPD art. 48.

7. Requests from authorities

The Provider holds no video, audio, screens, transcripts or documents of the Customer and therefore cannot hand them over to any authority. A request about that content must be addressed to the Customer.

8. Verification

The Customer can verify what this Annex states with the browser's network inspector and with the offline version. Upon written request to {{contato}}, the Provider will give the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate what this Annex states.

9. International transfer

The Provider does not transfer Customer content, because it does not receive it. The third parties in section 3 that the browser connects to may be located outside Brazil, and process the technical data of the connection under their own policies.

10. Who had an account before 4 November 2026

Earlier versions of this Annex governed the data of the paid account, kept at Supabase, with Stripe for billing and Brevo for sending e-mails. That system was shut down on 4 November 2026. Account data is erased when the database of the former system is deleted; invoices and tax records are kept for the period Brazilian law requires; and the confidentiality and erasure obligations of those versions remain in force until fulfilled.

11. Term and precedence

This Annex is in force while the Customer uses {{marca}}. In case of divergence between this Annex and the published Privacy Policy, the published version prevails.
