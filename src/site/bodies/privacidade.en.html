Summary

We do not receive your videos, your audio, your screens, your transcripts or the documents you generate. All of it is processed in your own browser, and no server of ours receives that content — the site has no application server: it is static pages plus the tool, hosted on Vercel.

There is no account, sign-in, registration or payment. The site sets no cookies.

What does exist is this, detailed below: the technical data any host receives with each request; an aggregate count of page views, without cookies; the downloads of libraries and models your browser makes when you use transcription, the PDF or text reading; Google, only if you click to use it; and the e-mails you decide to send us. And, until they are deleted, the records of the former account system.

How the service works

When you choose a video or record your screen, the content is read directly by the browser and stays on your device. Extracting the screens, reading the audio, transcribing and assembling the PDF, the Word file or the image package all happen entirely on your computer, using its processing power.

The file is not uploaded to us, and there is no temporary copy on any system of ours, because no system of ours is involved in the processing. You can check this with the browser's developer tools (F12) open on the Network tab.

The offline version — the single file you download — contains none of the external addresses described in this policy.

Data we do not collect

  • Videos, audio, extracted screens, transcripts and generated documents
  • File name, size, duration or any characteristic of what you process — unless you write it in an e-mail to us yourself
  • Name, phone number, password or any registration: there is no account
  • What you do inside the tool: we do not record usage events
  • Tracking identifiers, behavioural profiles or advertising data
  • Cookies: the site sets none — no session, tracking, advertising or measurement cookies

What is kept in your browser

So as not to ask for the same thing every time, the tool keeps some information in your own browser. It stays under your control, is not sent to us and we have no access to it. Clearing the site data in your browser erases everything described in this section. For the same reason, switching computer or browser means starting from scratch.

In local storage (localStorage), which lasts between visits:

  • preferences for use and display — for example, window sizes, the last transcription mode chosen and which panels were left open;
  • the templates you save (scenario, label, environment, system, layout, role);
  • the client brand — name and logo — and the “Issued by” field, if you fill them in;
  • the remembered vocabulary list, only if you tick the box to remember it. It comes unticked, and unticking it deletes the list;
  • small technical notes, such as which combination of library and format managed to open the transcription model on this computer.

In tab storage (sessionStorage), which the browser erases when the tab is closed: the session vocabulary, the system and executed by fields, the step script you paste, the evidence numbering counter and a technical summary of the last recording (counters, with no image and no text).

In the browser cache: the page files, kept by the service worker so that the tool opens without a network, and the files of the transcription model, so they need not be downloaded again. The delete the stored model button, next to the diagnostics, deletes the model and the technical note linked to it.

The defaults file (padroes-walkstamp.json) that the tool lets you export and import is a file you download and keep wherever you like: it carries only configuration — templates, brand, issuer and classification —, no recording and no evidence text, and it does not pass through us.

A note about links: the tool can be opened with fields already filled in through the address — for example ?caso=CT-014&chamado=NAT-1234. Whatever goes in the address stays in your browser history and in the hosting's access logs. A system name and a ticket number are fine; personal data is not — for that, type it on screen.

The screens of a recording in progress

This is the only part of your work the tool keeps for longer than the tab, and it exists for a concrete reason: a tab can crash, and a crash at the end of a long recording would wipe the whole recording.

So, while you record, each captured screen is also written to your browser's private file storage (the Origin Private File System). It stays on your computer, in an area only this page can read, and is not sent anywhere.

  • It deletes itself as soon as a document comes out. The moment you generate the PDF, the Word file, the .zip or any other output, the copy on disk is deleted.
  • It deletes itself after seven days, even if no document came out.
  • You can discard it at any time. When a recording is stored, step 1 says so and offers “discard” next to “bring back”.

Screen, microphone and webcam recording

When you use recording, the browser asks for your permission to capture the screen and, if you tick the option, the microphone. The image and the audio are processed on your own computer: the screens are extracted and the audio is transcribed right there. The continuous video of the session is not kept — the optional exception is the clips described below. Nothing from this capture is transmitted to us.

Webcam in a corner. Optional and off by default. With it ticked, the browser asks for permission to use the camera and the image is drawn in a corner of the screens and clips, on your computer. If you are going to film someone, ask that person for permission: we have no way of asking for you.

Clip of the marked moments. With the option on, and only for the moments you mark during the recording, a few seconds of video with sound around each mark are kept. These clips stay in this tab's memory: closing the tab erases them. They only reach your disk if you download the .zip package, and they pass through no server. The “discard the videos” button deletes them all at once and records the date of the discard in the document.

When you write to us

The tool's “Talk to us” button sends nothing by itself: it opens your own e-mail program, with a message already addressed to suporte@walkstamp.com. If you leave the box ticked — it comes ticked —, the beginning of a technical report of the browser goes along: technical data such as the browser version, its capabilities and figures from the last recording (how many screens, how long), with no image, audio or text from the recording. You see the report on screen first, and you see the whole e-mail in your program before sending. When the report does not fit in the e-mail, the tool copies it to the clipboard, for you to paste if you wish. To build the report, the tool tests whether the addresses of the transcription model respond — the same ones as in the table above.

When a video does not open, the “Tell me which format failed” link does the same: it opens your e-mail program, addressed to {{contato}}, with the file's extension and size and the browser — the file name does not go.

The “Send by e-mail” button, for recommending the tool to a colleague, also opens your e-mail program, with the site's address in the text. The recipient is whoever you choose, and the message does not pass through us.

If you send us an e-mail, we receive your e-mail address and what you wrote, including the report if it goes along, in our mailbox, hosted by HostGator. We use the message only to reply and to follow up the matter you raised: we do not use it for advertising, we do not combine it with other data and we do not share it with anyone. We keep the message for as long as that requires, and delete it sooner if you ask, through {{contato}}.

The blog

The blog is static pages: it has no comments, form or registration, and no tracking beyond the visit counting described above. The share buttons on each article are ordinary links, which only open LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X or your e-mail program when you click.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies — no session cookie, because there is no account, and no tracking, advertising or measurement cookie. Visit counting works without cookies, which is why there is no cookie banner on this page: there would be nothing to consent to. Google's windows, if you use Drive, belong to Google and follow its policy.

Who had an account before 4 November 2026

Until 4 November 2026 there was an optional account, tied to the paid plans. It signed in through a link sent by e-mail, kept data in a database at Supabase (São Paulo region, in Brazil), sent the sign-in and invitation e-mails through Brevo and charged subscriptions through Stripe. That system has been shut down: it is no longer possible to sign in, the paid plans are no longer offered and there will be no further charges.

What happens to the data of those who used that system:

  • Account data — sign-in e-mail, settings, templates, brand, scripts, cases, receipts, attachments, seats and support tickets — stays in the database, unused and with access restricted to us, until the database is deleted, and is erased when the database is deleted. The other records the database holds, such as the addresses of the former notification list and the anonymous usage counts, are erased with it.
  • Before the deletion, we make a backup copy of the database. It is kept with restricted access and used only to meet the legal obligations below and to answer data subject requests about those records.
  • Invoices and tax records are kept for the period Brazilian tax and accounting law requires. This is a legal obligation (LGPD art. 7, II), not a choice of ours or yours.
  • Payments were processed by Stripe, which keeps its own payment records under its own privacy policy. The card number never passed through us.

If you had an account and want a copy of your data, or want it erased before the database is deleted — except the tax records —, write to {{contato}}.

Information security

The main security measure is the architecture: the content you record does not leave your computer, so there is no copy of it on our side to protect, leak or hand over. The site and the tool are served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the site has no application server and no database of its own in use.

We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification, and we will not write that we do. The Security page describes how to check what the tool does.

Security incidents

On our side there are only the e-mails we receive and, until they are deleted, the records of the former account system. If a security incident occurs that may cause relevant risk or harm to data subjects, we notify the ANPD (Brazil's National Data Protection Authority) and the affected data subjects, under LGPD art. 48 — and, where applicable, the European supervisory authority, under the GDPR —, saying what is known: which data was affected, what has already been done and what we recommend.

Requests from authorities

We only hand data to an authority under an order with a valid legal basis, and we hand over only what was requested. We notify the person whose data was requested, unless the law forbids that notice. And what we do not have cannot be handed over: there is no video, audio or transcript in our possession. A request about the content of a recording must be addressed to whoever recorded it.

Where the data lives

Your content stays on your device. The e-mails you send us stay in our mailbox, at HostGator. The technical access data and the visit count stay at Vercel, whose network is global: they may be processed outside Brazil, which constitutes an international transfer under LGPD art. 33, made on the basis of the contractual safeguards offered by the provider.

The records of the former account system stay in the Supabase database, in the São Paulo region, until it is deleted; Stripe's payment records stay with Stripe.

Your rights

The LGPD (art. 18) guarantees you, at any time and free of charge:

  • confirmation that processing exists, and access to the data;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or out-of-date data;
  • anonymisation, blocking or erasure of unnecessary data or data processed unlawfully;
  • portability to another provider;
  • erasure of data processed on the basis of your consent;
  • information about whom we share data with;
  • information about the possibility of not consenting, and what that implies;
  • withdrawal of consent, at any time;
  • objection to processing carried out in breach of the law.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the GDPR guarantees equivalent rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection (arts. 15 to 21).

In practice, the only personal data of yours we hold is, at most, the e-mail you sent us — and, if you had an account before 4 November 2026, its records, until they are deleted. What is in your browser you erase yourself, by clearing the site data. And the content of your recordings is not with us: for that content, the rights are exercised with whoever recorded it.

To exercise any right, write to {{contato}}. We reply within 15 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the ANPD and, if you are in the European Economic Area, with the data protection authority of your country.
