The paid plans do not exist yet — they are listed to make clear what will be charged for and what never will be. Nothing here is for sale today, and the free plan will not lose features when the paid ones arrive.

Want to be notified?

There is no automated waiting list — it would be incoherent to build an email capture form into a tool that takes pride in collecting nothing. For now, follow the repository on GitHub: every update lands there first.

Why the free plan has no catch

It is a fair question: a free tool usually charges somewhere else — data, ads, or a limit that shows up at the worst moment. Here the reason is simpler: your video is processed by your own computer, so serving you costs practically nothing. There is nothing being subsidised, and therefore nothing to recoup later.
