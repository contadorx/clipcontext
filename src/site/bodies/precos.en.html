Questions from people who are buying

Can I try it before paying?
Yes. Anyone entering the account for the first time gets 14 days with everything in Team, no card and no checkout. When the 14 days end, nothing is charged: the account goes back to Free, and what you already produced stays on your computer.
Do I need a card to use Free?
No. No card and no account.
What happens if I cancel?
Access continues to the end of the period you already paid for. After that Free keeps working, and you export your templates and your scripts.
Do you receive my video?
No, on no plan. Video, audio and transcript are processed on your computer and are not sent anywhere.
How does invoicing work?
Stripe does the billing. The invoice follows the payment, and the link to it appears in your account once it exists.
Can I buy 3 seats and grow later?
Yes. The number of seats is adjustable in your account at any time.
Does the licence work offline?
It does. It is checked on your computer. In exchange, revoking a seat respects the 1-to-90-day window whoever administers it chose.
